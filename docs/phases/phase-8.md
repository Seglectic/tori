# Phase 8: Field Readiness

Phase 8 validates the Handheld v1 build for repeated real use at home and in
the shop.

## Progress Checklist

### Hardware Validation

- [ ] Select and document the enclosure shape and material.
- [ ] Validate camera placement, lens opening, and quick label framing.
- [ ] Validate USB/service access, mounting, and internal cable routing.
- [ ] Measure battery runtime during repeated scan, update, and ingest sessions.
- [ ] Validate display readability and control comfort in expected work areas.
- [ ] Validate reliable Wi-Fi operation during normal LAN use.
- [ ] Validate tolerance to shop conditions: coolant mist, chips, and gloved use.

### Exit Criteria

- [ ] Complete repeated scan and update sessions on battery.
- [ ] Confirm the screen, camera, and controls are practical for repeated use.
- [ ] Confirm the assembled device is serviceable and mechanically reliable.
- [ ] Confirm LAN connectivity is reliable enough for field use.

## Goals

- Turn the Handheld v1 prototype into a reliable daily tool.
- Keep the product language sleek, technical, compact, and restrained.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The device can complete repeated scan/update sessions on battery.
- The screen is readable in expected work areas, including shop lighting.
- Camera placement supports quick label framing.
- Controls remain comfortable during repeated quantity edits.
- Wi-Fi connectivity is reliable enough for normal LAN use.
